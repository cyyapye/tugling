a writable skill-directory grant. The report records the restored host launch
explicitly rather than mislabeling it as that different recovery.

Eleven attempts passed; seven remain failed and link to matching later passes.
Final accounting is 49 passing and 17 failed criteria across all attempts.

deliveries with task criteria and source, without raw traces or identity keys.
Their original judgments retain 12 visible failures and 16 process criteria

knew the runtime conditions; neutral labels did not conceal failures visible in
the replies and do not establish parent blinding or an enhancement comparison.

Observed builder usage totals 2,626,535 input tokens, including 2,154,368 cached,
and 50,009 output tokens. Reviewer and operator usage is excluded. These are
observed token counts, not billing totals. The cases do not establish unseen-task
transfer, coverage of every skill, or an advantage over Pstack.
